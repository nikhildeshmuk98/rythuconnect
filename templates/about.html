🌾 About Rythu Connect

Empowering Farmers with Smart Agriculture Technology

🌱 What is Rythu Connect?

Rythu Connect is a smart digital agriculture platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, communities, and government services in one place.

This platform helps farmers with real-time market prices, weather updates, AI crop support, disease detection, and a powerful agriculture marketplace.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to digitally empower every farmer in India by providing technology-driven solutions that increase productivity, reduce dependency on middlemen, and improve income.

We believe agriculture is the backbone of our nation, and technology should reach every farmer — from small villages to large markets.
